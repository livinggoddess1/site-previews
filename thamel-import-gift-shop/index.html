Our story

Started by Pranesh and Mina Maskey

Thamel Import Gift Shop was established in Fort Collins by Pranesh and Mina Maskey. The shop carries handmade goods from artisans in Nepal, Tibet, India and Indonesia.

Our sister store, Nepal Tibet Imports, is a short walk away at 129 South College Avenue.
