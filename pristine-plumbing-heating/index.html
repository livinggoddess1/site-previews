While you wait for us

  1. Shut off the main water valve.Usually where the line enters the house, near the meter.
  2. Switch off the water heater.It should not run with the supply closed.
  3. Open a cold tap downstairs.It drains what is left in the pipes.
  4. Take photos.Your insurer will ask for them.
