About threading

Why thread instead of wax?

Threading uses a twisted cotton thread to lift hair from the root, one clean line at a time. Nothing hot touches your skin and no chemicals are involved, which is why people with sensitive skin often prefer it.

It is also precise. Because the thread follows a line, your brows come out even and sharply defined.
