La Casa del Pan

Pan dulce✦Oaxacan kitchen✦Open every day

Conchas, pan de yema and warm rolls from early in the morning, with an Oaxacan kitchen under the same roof.

This is the second La Casa del Pan, opened in 2024 in the old Lopez Bakery building at 11th and Mitchell. Fill a tray with pan dulce to take home, or sit down for a tlayuda and a cup of champurrado.

A south side bakery

Still baking at 11th and Mitchell.

The Garcia family started La Casa del Pan on South Muskego Avenue and brought the same breads to Mitchell Street in 2024.

Around Day of the Dead we bake pan de muerto by the hundreds. The rest of the year, come in any day of the week.
