Deli Seoul

Bagels✦Korean breakfast sandwiches✦Since 2008

A Costa Mesa bagel shop since 2008, run by a mother and son, where the egg sandwich can come with Seoul steak, spicy pork, Spam or tofu.

Pick a bagel, pick what goes in it, and add Irene's Korean chili mayo if you like some heat. There are rice bowls and rice rolls too, coffee to go with it, and a patio where dogs are welcome.

Mother and son

A bagel shop first. The Seoul part came later.

Irene opened the shop in 2008 as a traditional bagel shop. Her son Jun joined her later, and the Korean fillings and sauces went on the menu.

We are at 1510 Adams Avenue, Suite B, with plenty of parking out front. We open at 7 every morning and close in the afternoon, so come early.
