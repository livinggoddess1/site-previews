Our story

A bagel shop first. The Seoul part came later.

Irene opened the shop in 2008 as a traditional bagel shop. When her son Jun joined her, the Korean fillings and sauces went on the menu, and the regulars never looked back.

We are still a mother-and-son shop, still baking early, and still happy to put chili mayo on whatever you order.

Good to know

Questions people ask

When are you open?

We open at 7 every morning and close in the mid-afternoon. Weekend closing times are a little earlier, so call if you are coming late in the day.

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Call (714) 435-0333 and it will be ready when you arrive.

Is there parking?

Yes, there is plenty of parking right out front.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes. Dogs are welcome on the patio.

Do you have anything without meat?

Yes. The Seoul Tofu and Egg sandwich, and plenty of bagels and spreads.
