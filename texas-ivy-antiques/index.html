Our story

Open since 1997. Reopened in 2023.

Texas Ivy Antiques was founded in 1997 by Florence Mildred Carroll in a 1920s home on Amarillo's stretch of Route 66.

A fire in August 2022 closed the shop. It was rebuilt and reopened in June 2023, and the doors have been open since.
