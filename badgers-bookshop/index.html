The shop

Down the stairs, behind the flower shop

Badger's has been in College View since 2015. Owner Will Lock has filled its garden-level rooms with thousands of secondhand novels and nonfiction.

It takes a moment to find: we are just off 48th Street, near the flower shop, down a short flight of stairs. Readers say it is worth it.
