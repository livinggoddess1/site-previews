Malachy's Saloon

Irish pub✦Darts, pool & live music✦Open until 1 am

A neighbourhood Irish pub on Granite Street with a full bar, a wall of dart boards and a pool table in the back.

Come in for a pint after work, a game on the TVs or a band at the weekend. We open at 11 in the morning, noon on Sundays, and pour until 1 am every night.

Friendly people, cold beer and a free dart board.

Malachy's is the kind of pub where the bartender says hello and somebody is always up for a game of darts.

There is parking, room for a group, and music most weekends. Call ahead if you want to know who is playing.
