Our story

From one booth to the whole building.

Jane started here as a vendor, selling her own finds from a single booth. When the previous owners retired, she took over the mall and gave it a new name.

Today forty-five vendors fill the space. Every booth is somebody's eye and somebody's weekends, which is why no two visits are the same.
