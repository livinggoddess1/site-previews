The mall

On Sixth Street since 1980

6th Street Antique Mall opened in 1980 on Historic Route 66. Today it holds thousands of square feet of antiques from more than 50 dealers.

It is one of the anchors of the Sixth Street Historic District, a mile of antique shops, cafes and Route 66 history.
