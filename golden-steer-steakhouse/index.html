Since 1969

The dining room used to be a dance floor.

The building opened in 1934 as an Irish pub and later became the Pink Clock Lounge. The Golden Steer opened here in 1969, and the dining room sits where the Pink Clock's dance floor was.

The same family has run the kitchen since 1997. We are open for dinner Monday to Saturday and closed on Sunday.
