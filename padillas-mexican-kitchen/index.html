Padilla's Mexican Kitchen

Since 1984✦Red or green✦Sopaipillas with every plate

New Mexican cooking on Girard since 1984: enchiladas, tamales and chile rellenos under red or green, with a sopaipilla on the side of every plate.

Bennie and Mary Padilla opened the kitchen in 1984, and their daughters run it today. We are open Monday to Friday from 11 to 7. Bring cash or a check. We do not take cards, and there is an ATM at the edge of the parking lot.

A family kitchen

Red or green. Either way, a sopaipilla comes with it.

Padilla's has been in the same strip of shops near Girard and Indian School for more than forty years, cooking the food Albuquerque grew up on.

Tacos, burritos and tostadas are on the menu by the piece if you are not after a full plate. We are closed Saturdays and Sundays.
