Winthrop's bookshop and cafe. Come in for a coffee on the way to work and stay for a chapter.

Susan and Suzanne, mother and daughter, have run the Depot since 2011. In July 2024 we moved next door to a bigger room at 9 Somerset Avenue, with more space for books, tables and plants.

A Winthrop local

Same Depot. Bigger room.

The Depot is where a lot of Winthrop starts its morning. The move gave us room to spread out, and the regulars came with us.

We are open early six days a week and closed on Sundays. If you are after a particular book, call and we will check the shelf.
