The store

A Midtown favourite

Love Me Two Times is at 1740 South Harvard Avenue in Florence Park. A Tulsa guide to thrift and vintage shops put it first on its list, and Discover Oklahoma featured the store in 2022.

Shoppers say they are greeted warmly at the door. Home goods fill the front and vintage clothing fills the back.
