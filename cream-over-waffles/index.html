Our story

From a food truck to a front door

C.O.W. started as a food truck selling ice cream over waffles around Amarillo. The shop at 201-M Westgate Parkway, in the former Marble Slab space, is its permanent home.

The ice cream is still made from scratch, and the menu has grown to include paninis and a kids' menu.
