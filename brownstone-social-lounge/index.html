Cocktails & bourbon✦Wings & small plates✦Music most nights

A small lounge on Water Street for a proper cocktail, a plate of wings and somewhere comfortable to sit with friends.

We have been downtown since 2017. Come for happy hour after work, stay for the DJ, or bring a group for karaoke or a live set. The kitchen sends out wings, flatbreads and plates made for sharing.
