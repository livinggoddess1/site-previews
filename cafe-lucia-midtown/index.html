Café Lucia

Coffee✦Breakfast all day✦By Piedmont Park

A neighbourhood cafe on the ground floor of Piedmont House, a short walk from the park, with good coffee and breakfast all day.

Order a latte and a bacon, egg and cheese on a croissant, find a booth, and stay as long as you like. There are plants everywhere, a bookshelf, a chess set and room to open a laptop.
